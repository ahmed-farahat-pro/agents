What's inside

Current features Available now

Everything below ships in the downloaded build — ready on first launch.

PAD

8 Performance Pads

Eight sounds ready to trigger live — Kick, Snare, Hi-Hat, Clap, Bass, Riser, Chop and Horn. Use the number row or the top keyboard row, no mouse needed. Toggle keyboard mode off to type normally.

Keys 1–8 Q W E R T Y U I 8 sounds Instant trigger
FX

6-Effect FX Rack

A full effects chain applied in real-time to any audio — pads, imports, or the sequencer. Every knob has a precision slider beneath it with a live value readout. Drag or click to adjust while the music plays.

Reverb 0–100% Delay 0–100% Filter 0–100% Distortion 0–100% Pitch ±12 st Speed 0.5×–2×
YT

YouTube Downloader

Paste any YouTube URL, hit Download. TiltDJ fetches the audio as MP3 using yt-dlp (auto-installed on first use), runs waveform analysis, and drops the track straight into your mixer — no browser, no manual steps.

Paste URL → MP3 Auto waveform scan Saves to ~/Documents/TiltDJ Downloads
MIX

Multi-Track Mixer

Load as many audio files as you want and play them all at the same time. Each track gets its own volume fader, a live waveform strip, a green checkmark once analysis is done, and a play/stop button — full desk-side mixing without a DAW.

MP3 WAV AIFF M4A Simultaneous playback Per-track faders
WAV

Waveform Visualizer

See your import as a waveform with a live moving playhead. Tap anywhere on the waveform to set a hot cue marker in that colour and jump back to it instantly — great for verse/chorus navigation without scrubbing.

Live playhead Hot cue markers Instant jump
SEQ

16-Step Sequencer

An 8-row × 16-column grid — one row per pad sound. Toggle steps on/off to build a one-bar pattern, set your BPM, hit Play and the loop fires in sync with whatever tracks are running in the mixer.

8 × 16 grid One bar loop BPM control Mixer sync
VOC

Voice FX & Drops

Type any phrase or pick from built-in presets and press P. macOS native speech engine reads it aloud over your mix in real-time — no cloud, no latency. Use it for shout-outs, intros, or live commentary over your beats.

Native macOS speech Zero cloud Key P to fire Custom phrases
KB

Full Keyboard Control

Every major action has a key binding. Fire pads, tweak BPM up or down one click at a time, raise or lower master volume, trigger a voice drop — all without touching the mouse. Perfect for heads-down study sessions.

Guide

How to use

Four sections — pick your starting point.

01 — Getting started

First launch

  • Open TiltDJ from the Applications folder
  • Right-click → Open on first launch to bypass Gatekeeper
  • Use pads 1–8 or Q–I to trigger sounds immediately
  • Adjust FX knobs to shape the sound in real-time
  • Hit Play to start the transport and step sequencer
02 — Importing audio

Add tracks to the mixer

  • Click ADD AUDIO and pick MP3, WAV, AIFF or M4A
  • Waveform analysis runs automatically on import
  • Press play on any track to add it to the live mix
  • Multiple tracks can play together — use the individual faders
  • Files are saved to ~/Documents/TiltDJ Downloads
03 — YouTube downloader

Get audio from YouTube

  • Paste any YouTube URL into the downloader field
  • Click DOWNLOAD and watch the progress bar
  • The MP3 is saved and added to your track list automatically
  • Waveform analysis starts immediately after download
  • Requires an internet connection (yt-dlp auto-installed)
04 — Using FX

Shape sound with effects

  • Drag FX knobs or use the sliders beneath each one
  • Reverb — add space and depth
  • Delay — create echo and rhythmic repeats
  • Filter — cut highs for that underwater feel
  • Distortion — add grit and saturation
  • Pitch — shift semitones up or down
  • Speed — slow down or double-time playback

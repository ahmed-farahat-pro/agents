What's inside PRO v2.0

One window. Full groove box.

No DAW needed — TiltDJ runs standalone on your Mac desktop.

PAD

8 Pads + keyboard shortcuts

Kick, Snare, Hat, Clap, Bass, Riser, Chop, Horn. Click or use 1–8 or Q W E R T Y U I while you work.

FX

6-effect FX rack

Reverb, Delay, Filter, Distortion, Pitch (±12 semitones), Speed (0.5×–2.0×) — all with interactive sliders and real-time visual feedback.

IMP

Import & remix

Add MP3, WAV, AIFF, M4A files. Each track shows waveform analysis, volume slider, and play/stop control. Delete when done.

SEQ

16-step pattern grid

8×16 grid — one row per pad. Toggle steps, set BPM, hit play and get a one-bar loop to jam over your import track.

CUE

Waveform + hot cues

Live playhead on the import waveform. Tap to drop hot cue markers and jump to any moment — quick arrangement in a single click.

MIC

Voice drops

Type a line or pick a preset — the app reads it aloud via native macOS speech. Zero cloud, instant shout-out over your beat.

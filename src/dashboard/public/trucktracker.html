Platform features

Everything a fleet owner needs

Track the vehicle, not the driver. Your GPS hardware sends location — TruckTracker shows you everything.

Live map — all vehicles

All your trucks shown as moving pins on one map. Exactly like Uber cars — you see them move, stop, and change direction in real time.

Automatic trip counting

Every time a vehicle starts, drives, and stops — it counts as a trip. No manual input. See trip history per vehicle, per day, week, or month.

Kilometer tracking

Total distance per vehicle: today, this week, this month, and all-time. See which vehicles are working hardest and plan maintenance accordingly.

Unusual stop alerts

When a vehicle sits idle for an unusual amount of time, you get an alert. Know before a problem becomes a crisis — delayed delivery, breakdown, or route deviation.

Fleet-wide dashboard

One summary view: how many vehicles are moving, stopped, or offline right now. Total trips and kilometers across your whole fleet today.

Push notifications

Get instant alerts on your phone when a vehicle stops unexpectedly, goes offline, or reaches a destination. Powered by Firebase — completely free to run.

Setup

Ready in three steps

No apps to install on your trucks. The hardware does the work — you just watch the dashboard.

1

Install a GPS tracker in each vehicle

A small device plugs into your truck. It has a SIM card inside and starts transmitting location automatically. Hardware is purchased once by the fleet owner — available in Egypt for approximately 1,350–2,700 EGP per unit including installation.

2

Register your fleet account and add vehicles

Create your owner account, add each vehicle's tracker ID, and give it a name. Your vehicles appear on the dashboard within minutes of adding them.

3

Open the dashboard and watch your fleet

From any browser or phone, open TruckTracker and see all your vehicles. No installation, no complex setup — just a URL and your login.

GPS tracker hardware — one-time cost, owner-purchased

The platform tracks the vehicle, not the driver. Each vehicle owner buys and installs one GPS tracker. The device connects to mobile data via an internal SIM and sends location pings to TruckTracker automatically — no interaction needed from the driver.

Choose your version

Standard refresh or fully live — your choice

Both versions track every trip and kilometer. The difference is how often the map updates.

Standard

Refresh every few minutes

Vehicle positions update every 2–5 minutes. Simpler infrastructure, lower operating cost. Ideal for most fleet owners who need visibility without the real-time requirement.

  • All trip and km tracking features
  • Stop alerts and notifications
  • Lower infrastructure cost
  • Positions refresh every 2–5 min
Live Real-Time

Continuous like Uber

Vehicle pins move on the map in real time — exactly like how Uber shows its cars. Requires a persistent MQTT connection per vehicle. Richer experience for owners who monitor actively.

  • Everything in Standard
  • Pins move continuously on map
  • MQTT persistent connection
  • Sub-second location updates

Target market

Built for fleet owners in Egypt and the Arab world

Practical businesspeople who need clear visibility over their vehicles — without complexity or enterprise contracts.

Truck fleet owners

5–200 heavy transport trucks operating between cities. Need to know where each vehicle is, how many runs it completed, and if anything is wrong.

Van and delivery fleets

Courier companies, furniture delivery, medical supply chains. Multiple daily trips across urban routes — trip counting per vehicle matters most.

Heavy equipment owners

Construction companies, agricultural machinery, tankers. Large high-value assets that move between sites — location and idle-time alerts are critical.

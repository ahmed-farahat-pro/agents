About Smart College

A Prestigious Heritage
in Heliopolis.

Set within a distinguished heritage landmark in the heart of Cairo's most prestigious neighbourhood, Smart College International Schools cultivates an environment where academic distinction, creativity, and character are seamlessly nurtured.

With just 20 students per class across 18 carefully curated classrooms, every student receives the personalised attention and mentorship they need to achieve their full potential and gain admission to the world's leading universities.

How the Admission Form Works in Phase 1

A parent visits the admissions page, fills in the form, and submits. They see a confirmation message on screen. At the same time, a full copy of their submission is emailed automatically to info@smartcollegeschool.com. Your admissions team reads the email, calls or emails the family, and handles everything from there. Clean, direct, and requires no dashboard or database in Phase 1.

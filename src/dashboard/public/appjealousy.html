AppJealousy — dock drama, on a timer

Pinned Dock apps remember how long you’ve ignored them. Stay in one app too long and the others get… emotional. Tune “jealous after N seconds” (default 10s) and watch the mood strip climb from calm to chaos — playful UI, not analytics.

Live metaphor · Safari in focus, neighbors restless

Jealousy threshold 3s — 180s

What it does

Reads apps in your Dock, watches the frontmost app, and nudges each icon’s mood from calm to jealous based on time spent elsewhere.

Dock + extras

Every pinned Dock app, plus any bundle you add with Add app to track (pick a .app).

Slider & persistence

Jealous after N s — 3 to 180 seconds. Your choice survives relaunch.

Notifications

Optional banners when an app feels neglected. Allow alerts in System Settings → Notifications → AppJealousy.

loved meh jealous full drama

Cheer / remove: Use the in-app actions to soothe jealous icons or drop added trackers from the list.

Install

Open the DMG, drag AppJealousy.app into Applications, then launch from the menu bar.

  • If Gatekeeper blocks the download, try xattr -cr ~/Downloads/AppJealousy-1.0.0.dmg and open again.
  • Rebuild locally with your bundle script: bash Scripts/package-appjealousy-dmg.sh (MacAppsBundle).

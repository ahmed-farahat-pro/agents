How Droob Works

Three interconnected workflows for instructors, students, and admins.

Instructor Workflow
Create. Monetize. Scale.

Sign up, choose a plan, and start building your course empire within minutes.

1

Sign Up & Verify

Join Droob with email or SMS. Submit your credentials for KYC verification. Approved instructors unlock full platform access instantly.

2

Select a Plan

Choose Free Trial, Basic, Pro, or Business. Each tier unlocks storage, student limits, and features. Upgrade anytime as you grow.

3

Build Your Course

Create sections and lessons. Add videos, PDFs, quizzes, and assignments. Write bilingual titles and descriptions. Preview everything before publishing.

4

Upload & Encode

Resumable uploads with real-time progress. Automatic encoding to HLS with 4 quality renditions. Live encoding status. Instant preview on completion.

5

Publish & Earn

Set your price. Publish instantly. Grade assignments. View analytics. Receive monthly payouts directly to your account.

Admin Workflow
Govern. Moderate. Scale.

Platform administrators keep Droob running smoothly with complete oversight and control.

1

Instructor Verification

Review KYC documents and commercial registrations. Approve or reject new instructors. Track verification status in real-time.

2

Plan Management

Create subscription tiers with custom limits. Set pricing, trial lengths, and feature flags. Update plans without affecting current subscribers.

3

Subscription Approvals

Review instructor upgrade, downgrade, and cancellation requests. Validate usage against new limits. Approve or reject with detailed notes.

4

Content Moderation

Review flagged courses. Manage content disputes. Monitor storage and bandwidth usage per instructor. Take action instantly when needed.

5

Financial Operations

Monitor revenue and commission. Approve monthly payout batches. View detailed financial reports. Manage refunds and chargebacks.
